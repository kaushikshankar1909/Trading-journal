TALLY
Combination: kam se kam 2 tagged candle (RB/IB/IS/RS) chahiye
Position % = (POC − Low) ÷ (High − Low) × 100. 0-25% Bottom, 25-75% Center, 75-100% Top.
RED+Bottom = RB (nakli) · RED+Center = IS (asli) · GREEN+Top = RS (nakli) · GREEN+Center = IB (asli)
Delta-match check (naya): Genuine (IB/IS) ke liye delta candle-color se MATCH hona chahiye (green candle→green delta). Mismatch ho to genuineness weak padti hai, RS/RB (fake) ka chance badhta hai. Coach: "delta should be green like price gone up but here red delta" — ye mismatch hi fake-pattern ka signature hai.
Delta filter (ab auto-enforce hota hai): Dominant delta 75K+ chahiye (coach LLT benchmark), kam ho to auto-suggest "Not useful" dikhega chahe pattern-shape match kare.
Imbalance boxes (alag concept, RB/IS/RS/IB se related nahi): Agar chart pe consecutive diagonal yellow boxes dikhein, unki ginti "CONSECUTIVE IMBALANCE BOXES" field mein daalo — 3+ ho to confirmed institutional-drive, 1-2 ko ignore karo.
"1 lakh POC volume" (alag concept — ye candle-tag filter nahi hai): Ye chart ki "extended volume POC" setting hai (yellow reference lines) jo key S/R levels highlight karti hai — agar "Exhaustion (POC extreme)" tag use kar rahe ho, apne chart pe check kar lo ki wahi POC us 1-lakh-highlighted level ke paas hai ya nahi, journal mein alag number nahi daalna.
Combination chahiye — RB+IB, IB+IB, RS+IS, IS+IS, RS+RB. Single pattern kaafi nahi.
